Desk Cam 4K

Button 16 selects Cisco Desk Cam 4K.

Use this as the fourth camera selection alongside the Quad Camera and RVPTZ cameras.

Stick buttons

Buttons 3 and 4 are the swap controls.

Either button swaps the live camera with the preview camera, so the operator can prepare a camera in Preview and then take it live quickly.

Enable Joystick Controls on the Touch Panel

Enable the controls and match the on-screen handedness to the physical switch before using the joystick.

  1. On the touch panel, open Joystick Controls.
  2. Under Joystick controls, tap Enabled.
  3. Select Left-handed or Right-handed to match the LEFT or RIGHT switch on the bottom of the joystick.
  4. Confirm the selected buttons are highlighted, then use the joystick controls described in this guide.

Tracking note: Enabling joystick control sets SpeakerTrack to Manual and turns off tracking modes. Disabling joystick control later does not restore those modes.

Operating Workflow

Use Preview as the staging area whenever possible. The safest habit is to prepare the next camera in Preview, take it live with a swap, then prepare the next shot.

10Prepare Preview

Press Preview before choosing a camera when you want to set up the next shot without changing what the room sees live.

12111516Pick Camera

Choose Quad Camera, RVPTZ Left, RVPTZ Right, or Cisco Desk Cam 4K.

34Take Live

After the Preview camera is framed, press either swap button. The prepared Preview camera becomes Main/live.

Standard operating loop

  1. Press Preview with 10 so the next camera can be prepared safely.
  2. Select the camera with 12 Quad, 11 RVPTZ Left, 15 RVPTZ Right, or 16 Desk Cam 4K.
  3. Frame the shot with the stick: push/pull to tilt, twist to pan, and use the center mini pitch circle to zoom.
  4. Fine tune by holding 1 while moving the camera slowly.
  5. Take it live with 3 or 4.
  6. Repeat by returning to Preview, choosing the next camera, positioning it, and swapping when ready.

When to control Main

  • Use 5 only when the live camera needs immediate correction.
  • Remember that Main movement is visible to the room.
  • Return to Preview with 10 before preparing the next shot.
  • Slider0 is not used in this workflow.
